## Easy Pressure Cooker Method
- **Pork butt**, cut into large chunks
- **Brown sugar, cayenne, paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, cumin, salt, pepper, etc.**
- **Cola**, beer, or stock
- **Barbecue sauce, hot sauce, etc.**
- *Serving suggestion:* In a butter-toasted sandwich roll, with corn on the cob and pickle spears

Marinade the pork butt. Sear all sides in the bottom of the pressure cooking pot. Deglaze and fill with a shallow layer of liquid to pressure cook with. Cook on high pressure for upwards of an hour until completely tender. Shred, season, and dress in sauce.

---
title: Grilled Cheese
description: "Easy and comforting melted cheese sandwich."
grade: E
updatedDate: 2025-07-17
---

- Sliced **bread**, not too thick
- Sliced **cheese**, or a mix, with at least one being a melting cheese
- Salty **butter**, or thinly spread mayonnaise
- *Options:* **Mustard, deli meats, pickles, hot peppers, onion, jams, etc.** Spread on bread or between slices of cheese.
- *Serving suggestion:* Tomato soup, cornichons

Optionally butter and toast the inside of each slice on a pan. Add cheese, close the sandwich, and butter/toast each side until golden brown. Add gentle pressure and keep the heat low enough so that the inside is melted when done.

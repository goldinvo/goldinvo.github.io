grade: D

- Kielbasa or similar sausage, lardons or bacon
- Pork and/or other meats, for stewing, in small pieces
- Sauerkraut and shredded green cabbage
- Onions, mushrooms, and other vegetables
- Bay, allspice, pepper, caraway, marjoram, juniper berries, paprika, garlic, tomato paste, dried porcini mushrooms, etc.
- *Optional for sweetness*: prunes, apple, sugar or honey, raisins
- Stock or water, red wine (opt.)

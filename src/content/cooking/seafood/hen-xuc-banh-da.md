---
title: Hến Xúc Bánh Đa
description: "Vietnamese stir-fried baby clams served on rice crackers."
grade: E
updatedDate: 2025-08-08
---

- **Thịt Hến** (baby clam meat), pre-cooked or blanched/steamed, rinsed thoroughly of sand and pressed dry, optionally washed in michiu/rice wine. Should be dry and fluffy.
- **Garlic, shallot, lemongrass**, chili (opt.). All minced.
- **Fish sauce, sugar, pepper**, chicken powder or msg, etc.
- **Rau răm** (Vietnamese coriander), roughly chopped
- *For serving:* **Bánh đa mè đen** (black sesame rice cracker)

Fry aromatics in a bit of oil until fragrant. Add baby clam meat and stir-fry, cooking off any remaining liquid and adding seasonings.  Stir in herbs while still hot until slightly wilted. Serve, scooping it up on rice crackers.

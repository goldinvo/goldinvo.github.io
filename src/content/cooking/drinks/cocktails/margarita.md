---
title: Margarita
description: Tequila, Cointreau, lime juice, simple.
grade: D
updatedDate: 2024-10-26
---

- 2 oz. Tequila blanco
- 3/4 oz. Cointreau
- 3/4 oz. (or 1 lime) Lime juice 
- 1/4 oz. Simple syrup
- *For the glass:*
    - Lime wedge
    - Salt

Salt the rim of a rocks glass with a wedge of lime. Add 1 large ice cube.

Shake with ice. Strain.
